We're glad to share that Amritanshu Aditya, Chair of the SRM University ACM Student Chapter, has been appointed Regional Coordinator for Region 4 i.e. Tamil Nadu & Kerala with ACM India.

ACM India supports 195+ student chapters nationwide, and the coordinator's role is to make sure the chapters in a region aren't working in isolation — helping them run stronger activities, surface their work, access ACM's resources, and build with each other rather than alongside each other. It's a natural extension of the work Amritanshu has been leading here.

An open note to every ACM Student Chapter in Tamil Nadu and Kerala: whether it's a question about chapter operations, membership, event planning, reporting activities, awards, or a collaboration you'd like to explore, please reach out. No query is too small.
